Pre-Query Intent

Independent notes on AI judgment, gateways, and orchestration above models.

As artificial intelligence expands across models, agents, tools, search, devices, and services, an architectural question becomes increasingly important: what decides which intelligence should be invoked before an answer or action occurs?

This question connects pre-query intent, context, judgment, multi-model orchestration, agent selection, AI gateways, control planes, interoperability, and ecosystem neutrality.

As models and agents proliferate, competition may extend beyond the capability of individual models toward the layer that determines which model, agent, search system, tool, or service should act in a given context.

The following essays examine this emerging architectural layer from two related perspectives: what happens before a question is fully formed, and what happens above increasingly numerous models and agents.
